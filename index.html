Who we are

Corai is an AI transformation and automation partner for businesses that want to move faster, without the overhead of building everything in-house.

We combine strategy, design, and engineering to deliver end-to-end solutions: from brand and digital presence to workflow automation and back-office integration. Our team works as an extension of yours, so you get production-ready outcomes without the hiring lag.

We are UK-based and work especially well with teams in regulated and high-trust sectors (including healthcare) where accuracy, compliance, and clear handoffs matter.

Whether you need a new website, smarter processes, or a full AI roadmap, we focus on clarity, delivery, and results you can measure. Detailed case studies are on the way. On a call we can share relevant examples and references.
